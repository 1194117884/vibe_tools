Sidebar Toggle — Which collapse style?

When the sidebar is collapsed (hidden to the left), how should the page look and feel? Click to select.

A

Icon-only rail (Recommended)

Collapsed sidebar shrinks to a narrow icon strip (~56px). Icons remain visible for quick navigation. Hover or click toggle to expand back to full width. Content area gets more space.

B

Full hide

Sidebar completely slides off-screen to the left. A small floating toggle button stays visible to bring it back. Maximum content space when collapsed.

C

Overlay on hover

Sidebar collapses to a thin edge (~4px). Hovering over the edge expands the sidebar as an overlay on top of content (no layout shift). Click toggle to lock it open.
